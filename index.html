About this practice ballot

This guide covers Grand Rapids and nearby Byron Township, East Grand Rapids, Gaines Charter Township, Grand Rapids Charter Township, Grandville, Kentwood, Walker, and Wyoming. Enter a ZIP code to narrow the precinct list. A ZIP code usually contains more than one precinct, so you still choose the precinct. State House, State Senate, school board, and district court contests are the ones Kent County assigned to that precinct for November 3, 2026. A split precinct lists every district that includes part of it. Walker and Grandville are served by the 59th District Court, which has no judge on this ballot. It is a reference guide, not a certified sample ballot. Byron Township’s parks millage is not included. County commissioner, the township board, Kent ISD, and Kent District Library have no contest on the November 3, 2026 county list.

Names, vote limits, and proposal wording come from the Kent County Official Candidates and Proposals List for the November 3, 2026 general election, updated October 21, 2026 at 2:05 p.m. Biographies are short summaries of the linked public sources, not endorsements. If a reliable biography was not found, the profile says so. Street addresses and personal email addresses from the filing list are left out.

The county list has some unusual entries that are left as printed, including a filing date later than the list’s update time, a school-board partial-term heading also labeled “6 Year Term,” and qualified write-in filings dated October 22.
